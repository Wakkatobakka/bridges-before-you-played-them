Not observed: physical phone launch/touch comfort/fit, audible phone playback, live successful Downloads export on a phone, phone heat, a full playthrough or later minigames. The save serializer was directly invoked on existing native score fields to test its genuine original file path; this does not establish a naturally earned completed-level save.
