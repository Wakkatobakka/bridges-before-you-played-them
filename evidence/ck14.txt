This test executes the actual guest `c.t` decoder, `c.j` byte wrapper, `c.e`
bitmap glyph mapper and `c.l` pager. Its text corpus oracle is independently
decoded from the original resources by Python's strict CP932 decoder.

The untranslated JAR reproduces the phone video's seven-line lobby description
and three-line dialogs with replacement glyphs. The separate compatibility JAR
must produce five lobby lines and two dialog lines, preserve all 340 strings in
six resource tables, leave resource bytes unchanged, and retain ASCII glyph
indices and `$P` pager behavior.

These are structural checks against real guest methods. The Java graphics
doubles do not reproduce Android Japanese font rasterization, so the test does
not certify physical handset text appearance or claim screenshots of that output.
