Observed on SM-S938U / Android 16:
- The v0.2.7 GL readback region around Rockman's predicted body contained 2,992 non-black GLES pixels out of 4,704.
- The Android bitmap merge copied all 2,992 of those pixels.
- Device video visibly showed changing GL-derived white/green/magenta content inside the CPU ROCKMAN region while the user interacted.
- Runtime remained free of GL errors and caught game exceptions.

Conclusion:
- GLES is genuinely rasterizing live Rockman/Figure pixels.
- The CPU screen mapping and OpenGL bottom-left -> Android top-left conversion are both working.
- The missing presentation boundary is bridged successfully by readback -> AndroidPlatform.back.
- The v0.2.7 Rockman-only readback rectangle can now be widened to a full gameplay 3D layer instead of continuing geometry/presentation probes.
