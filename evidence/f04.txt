## Figure model-unit pass

v0.0.16 proved that Cloud is animated, textured, pattern-filtered, and still projected thousands of pixels across a 240x240 framebuffer. The device report also shows the course/Primitive geometry living in a much smaller and sane world-coordinate range.

Snowboarding's own bytecode converts its internal world translations to DoJa Transform floats with `>> 6` (26.6-style units) before `Graphics3D.setTransform`. MBAC Figure geometry, however, was still being submitted by the bridge as raw model integers. v0.0.17 therefore applies a **Figure-only 1/64 model-to-world conversion after MTRA skeletal deformation and before the game-supplied world/view matrix**.

Dynamic Primitive terrain is intentionally unchanged. Camera convention, +Z projection, Z buffer, textures, MTRA animation, appearance-pattern filtering, controls, scratchpad and report output are unchanged.

New Figure telemetry includes `unitScale=1/64`, raw pose bounds, scaled world bounds, projected bbox and view-Z range.
