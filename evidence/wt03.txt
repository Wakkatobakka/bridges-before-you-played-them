The original tries optional s.ani/s.bgs splash animation, catches their absence,
and falls back to intact /s PNG screens. Those two assets are absent in the
original JAR. Only corresponding caught NullPointerExceptions are classified as
known optional-asset observations, not runtime faults. All other faults retain
stack evidence and automatic report export.

Native MIDI bytes go unchanged to Android MediaPlayer, including volume and
background pause/resume. Audibility/timbre on a physical phone is unobserved.
Host audio capture preserves requested bytes rather than claiming handset sound.
