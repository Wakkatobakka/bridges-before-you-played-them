compiled into the APK. Install over v0.1.0; package, signing and game-save

simultaneous cardinal contacts are preserved. WHIP / OK taps whip/hook;
hold for Fire. 1/2/3 retain left/vertical/right jump inputs. L SOFT, R SOFT
and CLR / BACK remain original keys. Controller mappings are preserved.

without pausing, restarting or replacing the renderer. Keypad mode retains
the distinct square D-pad, primary action, CLR and the complete original
numeric/softkey cluster. Owned touch inputs release safely while physical
holds survive switching. Minimum control width 312dp; panel height 204dp
normal and 272dp keypad. Every key >=48dp; pad 144dp square.
