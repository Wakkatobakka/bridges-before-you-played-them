Dragon Sword: original English BREW release, Minor Axis / SK Telecom and SkyZone Entertainment. Original game code, graphics and sound belong to their respective owners. The launcher icon is a new AI-generated illustration based on the original blond hero and red scarf; it is not official developer artwork.

Compatibility layer: Wakka ARM/Thumb BREW runtime, based on the private Earthworm Jim / Contra 4 / Doom RPG lineage. Dragon Sword adds its original IHeap ABI, posted startup events, character-name TextCtl services and string conversion/filter services. It executes the original unchanged ARM module.
