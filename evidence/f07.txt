## Perspective Y-orientation pass

v0.0.17 proved the Figure 1/64 model-to-world conversion: Cloud now occupies a sane on-screen footprint (roughly tens of pixels rather than thousands), but the device recording shows Cloud and the perspective course geometry vertically inverted.

The bridge had an internal projection inconsistency:

- parallel projection mapped `+viewY` to `+screenY`
- perspective projection mapped `+viewY` to `-screenY`

On the device, this made Snowboarding's mostly-negative-Y course geometry appear above the horizon while Cloud's mostly-positive-Y Figure geometry appeared head-down. v0.0.18 changes only the perspective screen-Y mapping so it matches the DoJa/Canvas downward-positive screen convention already used by the parallel path:

`screenY = centerY + viewY * focal / viewZ`

The change is applied to both Figure and Primitive perspective projection. Figure scaling, MTRA animation, pattern filtering, textures, camera transforms, +Z view convention, Z buffer, terrain generation, controls, storage and reporting are unchanged.

Telemetry remains enabled so the device report can confirm Cloud's bbox/depth after the orientation correction.
