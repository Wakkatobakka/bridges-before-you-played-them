WHAT STILL NEEDS TESTING / KNOWN LIMITS
- Physical phone startup, speed, heat, touch comfort, audio, background/resume,
  Downloads export and gameplay through later scenes have not been verified.
  Host instruction counts and virtual clocks are not phone performance results.
- QCELP effects are passed to Android as QCP. Decoder availability can vary by
  phone; successful desktop decoding does not prove Android playback. Errors
  are recorded. Original MIDI is passed unchanged to Android's synthesizer.
- Android system sans substitutes for the unavailable optional font extension;
  text metrics and raster edges can differ from the original handset.
- Wonderland gameplay is absent from this original ZIP. Journal/splash material
  does not make it a playable chapter. No replacement or automatic skip exists;
  a complete normal campaign through that missing chapter is not possible here.
- Current Omni hosted import/remove/reimport with save preservation, and two
  cumulative production public exports, remain acceptance checks. The included
  independent contract audit is not a substitute for those host/phone checks.
