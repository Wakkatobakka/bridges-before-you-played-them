Reporting follows RE4 Zeebo v0.1.9 ReportStore: immutable cached native metadata
at most once per 5 foreground seconds, plus faults; no framebuffer clone for
sampling. Manual export reads the volatile cache and queues an independent
writer; it never posts to or locks the guest. File saving uses MediaStore
Downloads on Android 10+, with private-external Documents fallback on 8/9.
Detected faults automatically export a FAULT TXT. Heavy instructions traces
remain off during play. See tests/report for busy-worker/session-lock coverage.
