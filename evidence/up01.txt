FRAME SCHEDULING / BACKGROUND
The original t.run() sets a timestamp once before the loop and subtracts
that same timestamp from its 33 ms sleep budget forever. Consequently its
sleep reaches the original 5 ms minimum even while idle on a fast host.
Platform.pace() enforces the original intended ~30 Hz budget without spin
waiting. Original methods, timers and movement logic remain untouched.
The original loop emits a unique curLoop: line each iteration. A private
PrintStream hook uses that existing boundary for pacing and foreground
scheduling while dropping repetitive loop spam. This is functional code;
do not remove it as cosmetic logging cleanup. Other original log lines and
stderr are retained in bounded diagnostics. Android backgrounding calls the
original MIDlet pause at this boundary and parks the worker on a condition;
foregrounding resumes the original MIDlet. No thread suspension or polling
spin is used. The host harness verifies native MIDlet pause/resume; Android
foreground parking still needs device testing.
