Normal controls follow the original active screen:
- Title: large START action.
- Menus: arrows and large SELECT action.
- Briefings/tips: large CONTINUE action.
- Original item/confirmation dialogs: PLAY / TAKE SMS or YES / NO.
- Live level/map: original movement, strafe, fire, grenade and map deck.
PLAY is prominent on the optional detector offer. TAKE SMS and digit 5 retain
the original optional-service action. NO leaves the SMS loop.
Original SMS/ranking services are unavailable; no network/SMS permissions
are requested.

ORIGINAL KEYPAD / D-PAD RETURN swaps controls inline. Both layouts show live
original commands. Keypad 5 says START, SELECT, CONTINUE, TAKE SMS, YES or FIRE
as appropriate. Original codes and physical bindings are retained.
Context changes release touch contacts; registered physical holds retain
their owners. The renderer continues without resetting or altering the game.

D-pad UP/DOWN moves forward/backward; LEFT/RIGHT turns during gameplay.
1/7 strafe right/left,5 fires/acts,* throws,# accesses map/search.
The original help incorrectly lists some movement numbers; labels follow
actual original EXN/platform execution. Exact digits and both softkeys remain
in keypad mode. Gameplay right softkey is QUIT MENU.
