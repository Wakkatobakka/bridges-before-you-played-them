v0.2.4 changes one conceptual layer:
1. Restore view*object composition.
2. Treat preserved host lookAt as already GLES -Z eye space (no second axis flip).
3. Render decoded Figure vertices at 1/64 model-unit scale.
4. Simultaneously log candidate frustum coverage at 1/32, 1/64, 1/100 and 1/128, plus raw/scaled model height and camera-to-object distance.
5. Keep textures, animation, D4D world decoding and audio unchanged.

Why 1/64 for the rendered probe:
- It is a compact binary unit conversion consistent with packed integer model storage.
- Applying it to the v0.2.3 recorded camera/object matrices places all four known Rockman body meshes inside the 60-degree view in an offline check.
- It is explicitly a probe, not a claim that 1/64 is the final format-wide scale. The on-device candidate telemetry and visual size will decide the next step.
