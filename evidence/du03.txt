Duke Nukem Mobile 3D
Version $VER$
 
 -- Controls --
 
Control pad - Movement
5 & OK - Fire
1 & 3 - Previous/next weapon
4 & 6 - Turn left/right
2 & 8 - Move forward/back
7 & 9 - Strafe left/right
 
Feature Information:
 
Enemy Alert causes blinking arrows to appear when enemies are nearby.
 
Quick Look allows you to instantly turn towards an enemy when red arrows are flashing by tapping left or right twice.
 
Note: Quick Look is only functional if Enemy Alert is also enabled.
