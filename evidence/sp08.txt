WHAT CHANGED / WHAT IS ORIGINAL
All three original inputs, native interpreter core, shared UI kit v1.1.2 source,
launcher artwork and private signing key are byte-identical to v0.1.1. The VM
frontend adds a read-only observer for original scene and episode fields. Java
adds episode controls, corrected Android audio output, and richer diagnostics.
Game code, graphics, original logic, timing and progress are not modified.
Original SMS services have no live backend and are not represented as successful.
