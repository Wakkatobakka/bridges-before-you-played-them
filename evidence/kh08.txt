Native timers keep their original deadlines. Input, reports and media callbacks
do not pump extra game ticks. Quick releases may wait only until one naturally
due callback has seen the press; lifecycle releases are immediate. The 500M
per-callback instruction limit is a fault ceiling for heavy scene loads, not a
performance target. Virtual clocks and guest-memory inspection are host tests.

The original optional font extension is unavailable; system sans is a disclosed
fallback. CMX WAVE effects are unwrapped without changing QCELP packets and
passed as QCP to Android. Do not claim phone playback without device evidence.
Original MIDI is unchanged. No replacement soundtrack or looping under levels.
