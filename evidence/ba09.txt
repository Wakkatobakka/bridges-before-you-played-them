BiaControls preserves the original four-way D-pad and a separate center OK code. Do not
invent analog/diagonal mappings. Both normal and original keypad modes retain the D-pad.

duplicate aliases and separate hardware input owners. Shared left/right actions provide
L SOFT, R SOFT and CLR alongside the numeric cluster without a redundant fifth row.
Android system Back calls real close/worker cleanup and returns to the launching task;
CLR is separately accessible as the original pause key. A mode switch is touch-only cleanup.

Do not change original SetTimer deadlines. The existing short-tap queue retains a key down
through a naturally due callback; no input or report operation advances timers.
