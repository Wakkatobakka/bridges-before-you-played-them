S09 — The original game chooses four-way or numeric movement

DeadShot's own key_con option controls movement family:
  modes 0 / 2 = four-way DoJa D-pad movement
  modes 1 / 3 = numeric-keypad movement, including diagonals

v0.0.5 touch behavior:
- The movement disc reads DeadShot's current key_con value and automatically emits
  the correct original movement family.
- Numeric modes expose eight 45-degree movement sectors; D-pad modes remain four-way.
- The action area exposes the actual *, # and OK/Select keys. No A/B/C aliases.
- Soft1 and Soft2 are exposed as MENU and BACK-style pill buttons; if DeadShot supplies
  a short soft-label string, the host displays that original label instead.
- Multi-touch and oversized invisible hit targets remain enabled.
