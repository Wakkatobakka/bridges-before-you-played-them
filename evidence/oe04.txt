CONTROLS — GROUNDED IN THE ORIGINAL GAME'S HELP TEXT
Original D-pad: Up/Down move forward/back, Left/Right turn.
5 / OK: attack, talk, interact.
1 / 3: strafe left / right.
7 / *: next / previous weapon.
9: pass turn. #: map. 0: game menu / back. CLR: previous screen.
The KEY PAD button reveals all 12 original numeric keys; nothing is remapped
inside the actual game. The Android presentation follows the REGenesis phone-style
layout while preserving Orcs & Elves II's different native controls.
