WHAT WAS REUSED
Ashen v0.1.3's unmodified Android EKA2L1 core, JNI callbacks, lifecycle,
first-install save protection and independent reporting. Its Pocket Kingdom/
Shadowkey runtime lineage is retained in provenance. The original N-Gage
Symbian 6 firmware was replaced with the required Nokia 5320 Symbian 9.3 FP2
files and N-Gage 2.0 service dependencies. This is a newer platform generation.
Actual Wakka Bridge Gameplay UI Kit v1.1.2 source is compiled into the APK.
Its source/JAR/tests are preserved in app/ui-kit/.
