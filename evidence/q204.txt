## v0.1.1 phone loading repair

Both supplied phone reports stop at the production compressed-texture guard:
`target=0xDE1 level=1 format=0x8C92 dimensions=32x64 size=1024`. The existing
ATC RGB decoder accepts those bytes; an inherited `level != 0` guard rejected
them first. v0.1.1 permits levels 0 through 11 within the existing bounded
2048-pixel texture contract and preserves the original level passed to GLES.
Payload length validation and invalid-level rejection remain active.

The former desktop renderer used a separate texture adapter and an empty
extension fixture, so it exercised the game's uncompressed fallback. Current
tests reproduce the exact failure against the archived v0.1.0 Android adapter,
then exercise the actual AndroidEglDriver.invokeGles. Test-only EGL/GL
service sinks keep this separate from physical Android acceptance. Full guest
play and reload runs advertise the explicit GL_ATI_imageon_misc capability
fixture, route every compressed upload through the production Android adapter,
and submit its decoded bytes to real Mesa OpenGL. Those v0.1.1 runs passed 1,851
compressed uploads including 1,612 nonzero mip levels and five matches of the
reported 32x64 level-1 shape. This source and service-shim change is test-only;
no Android framework replacements are packaged in the APK.
