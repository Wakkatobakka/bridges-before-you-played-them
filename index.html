For the first visit

Old phone games.
Another chance.

A human/AI workshop, one game at a time.

I wanted old phone games running on my Android phone. A bridge gives an original game the runtime, graphics, sound and controls it needs to run there. Omni Player brings those bridges together in one Android app; players supply the original game files.

I bring the ideas and constraints, test on my phone, and tell ChatGPT what still feels wrong. ChatGPT helps investigate failures and implement the next attempt. We put this archive together from the saved reports, checkpoints and my memories of doing it.

These chapters follow particular builds and moments in that work. For current releases and play instructions, head to Omni Player ↗. Here, start with a game you recognize—or with Cloud finally appearing upside down.

Across the collection

The pile becomes LEGION.

One game gives us a model parser. Another helps fix a timer. I picture the bridges stacked together, filling gaps in one another. The checkpoints show where those pieces actually traveled.

A note about the record

The rough edges
stay in the story.

A passing build, a running game and a good time holding the phone are three different milestones.

This archive follows the original games, the compatibility repairs and my phone testing. We kept the failed approaches, mismatched version labels and gaps in the record alongside the things that worked.

Game code, translations, upstream runtimes and the bridge work each keep their own credit. English Lost Episode patch integration, for example, is credited to TurquoiseHammer / Yuvi.

01

Phone reports

Observed counters, errors and events from a recorded device session. They establish that session’s behavior, with the report’s limits.

02

Host and build checks

Useful evidence for code paths, compatibility, packaging and regression checks. Their scope remains separate from physical-phone acceptance.

03

Owner feedback

A recorded acceptance or observation belongs to the build and behavior actually tested. A later candidate keeps its own open questions.

Published evidence is selected and reviewed. Full private checkpoints and implementation material remain outside this site. This is a history archive; play and release information lives with Omni Player ↗.
